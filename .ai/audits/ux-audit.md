# UX Audit

## Must Pass

- User can finish qixi reservation in under 1 minute.
- User can finish custom reservation in under 1 minute.
- The required form fields are visually clear.
- The no-payment message appears near form and success state.
- The WhatsApp next step is clear.
- Product card selection is obvious.
- Sticky CTA does not cover the submit button on mobile.

## Fail Conditions

- User cannot understand what happens after submitting.
- User thinks they have paid when no payment exists.
- User has to scroll through excessive content before reserving.
